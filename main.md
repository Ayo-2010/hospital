    HOSPITAL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM — FULL PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT PROMPT<br /><br />1.
    PROJECT OVERVIEW<br /><br />Build a modern, secure, scalable, responsive
    Hospital Management System (HMS) for a professional hospital/medical
    center.<br /><br />The platform must combine:<br /><br />1. A public-facing
    hospital website.<br />2. A secure patient portal.<br />3. A doctor/medical
    staff portal.<br />4. A nursing dashboard.<br />5. A surgery department
    dashboard.<br />6. A Head of Surgery dashboard.<br />7. A laboratory
    dashboard.<br />8. A pharmacy dashboard.<br />9. A billing/finance
    dashboard.<br />10. An administrator dashboard.<br />11. A hospital
    management/executive dashboard.<br />12. A notification and communication
    system.<br />13. A centralized electronic medical record system.<br /><br />The
    system should feel like a real-world enterprise healthcare product rather
    than a simple CRUD dashboard.<br /><br />The interface should be:<br /><br />-
    Professional<br />- Clean<br />- Trustworthy<br />- Modern<br />-
    Accessible<br />- Calm<br />- Medical/healthcare oriented<br />- Highly
    responsive<br />- Easy for non-technical hospital staff to use<br />-
    Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile<br />- Consistent across all
    dashboards<br /><br />Do NOT make every dashboard look identical. Maintain
    one design system while adapting navigation, statistics, tables, actions,
    and workflows to each role.<br /><br />---<br /><br />2. DESIGN DIRECTION<br /><br />Use
    a modern healthcare visual language.<br /><br />Primary colors<br /><br />Use:<br /><br />-
    Medical blue as the primary color<br />- Deep navy for important text<br />-
    White for surfaces<br />- Very light blue/gray backgrounds<br />- Green for
    successful/healthy states<br />- Amber/orange for warnings<br />- Red for
    critical/danger states<br />- Purple or teal for selected specialized
    departments<br /><br />Avoid excessive gradients.<br /><br />Avoid overly
    rounded "startup dashboard" aesthetics.<br /><br />Use moderate border
    radii, subtle shadows, clean borders, generous spacing, and strong
    typography.<br /><br />The design should communicate:<br /><br />«Trust +
    Safety + Professionalism + Efficiency + Technology»<br /><br />---<br /><br />3.
